# 🎮 Welcome to City Quest

**City Quest** is a small-scale **open-ended 3D city exploration and survival game** developed as a 5th-semester group project for **Human-Computer Interaction & Computer Graphics**.

The idea was simple:

> Give the player a city, let them explore it freely, and give them a reason to keep going.

Inspired by the freedom and exploration of open-world games such as *GTA Vice City*, the project creates a smaller academic-scale experience where the player can explore the city, search for hidden treasure chests, collect coins, purchase food, and keep themselves alive.

There is no forced traditional ending.

**The journey ends when the player decides to stop.**

#  Survival System

Exploration alone isn't enough.

The player must also manage their ability to survive by using collected coins to purchase food.

This creates a simple but meaningful gameplay relationship:

> **Explore → Earn → Spend → Survive → Explore Again**

The goal isn't simply to collect everything.

It's to make enough resources to keep your journey going.

Don't just follow a path.

##  02 — Search

Keep your eyes open for treasure chests hidden around the environment.

You never know what you might find.

##  03 — Collect

Discovering treasure gives you access to coins.

Coins become your most important resource.

##  04 — Spend

Use your coins to purchase food.

Because exploration means nothing if you can't survive it.

##  06 — Repeat

Go back into the city.

Find more.

Explore further.

Stay alive longer.

#  Game Design

City Quest was designed around a simple principle:

> **Give players a world and let curiosity drive them.**

Rather than building the project around a complicated storyline, the game focuses on:

* Exploration
* Discovery
* Resource collection
* Resource management
* Survival
* Player freedom

This makes the experience more open-ended and allows the player to decide how they want to approach the game world.

The environment therefore becomes part of the gameplay rather than simply being decoration.
